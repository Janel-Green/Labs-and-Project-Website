My Preferred Streaming Services

Netflix

Netflix offers a huge variety of movies and show, and even games. They also have documentaries and a vast majority of series.

Most of my favorite shows and movies are available on Netflix.

Visit Netflix

Hulu

Hulu offers a wide range of TV shows and movies, including original content. It's a great option for those who enjoy both popular and niche programming.

Some of my favorite movies are available on Hulu.

Visit Hulu

Disney +

Disney+ offers a vast library of Disney, Pixar, and Marvel content. It's perfect for fans of these franchises. You can also access other streaming services through Disney+, such as Hulu.

Some of my favorite movies are also available on Disney+.

Visit Disney+

Peacock

Peacock offers a wide range of TV shows and movies as well. It's a great option for those who enjoy both popular and niche programming. I also enjoy Peacock's reality TV shows specifically.

Some of my favorite reality TV shows are available on Peacock.

Visit Peacock

HBO Max

HBO Max offers a large sum of HBO content, including original series and movies. It's a great option for fans of premium television programming. I use HBO Max the least out of all of these services, but I still use it every now and then.

Only a few of my favorite shows are available on HBO Max.

Visit HBO Max

Prime Video

Prime Video offers a wide selection of movies and TV shows, along with exclusive content. It's a great option for Amazon Prime members who want to stream their favorite content. I use Prime Video very often as well.

Some of my favorite shows and movies are also available on Prime Video.

Visit Prime Video
