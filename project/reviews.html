Reviews of My Favorite Shows and Movies!

Here are some of my favorite shows and movies that I feel are really interesting to watch, as well as their ratings! When it comes down to a rating, my rating focuses on the acting, plot or storyline, and the actual entertainment value.

TV Show Reviews

A Different World

Genre: Sitcom

Rating: 8.5/10

This is my most recent favorite show to watch. The acting isn't exactly the best, but the storyline represents the realities of college that many students face. Last but not least, the entire cast was very attractive. So it's actual entertainment level is quite high due to the cast members.

My Wife and Kids

Genre: Sitcom

Rating: 8/10

This is one of my past-time shows. This is a show that you can watch or just have in the background for noise. It's extremely funny so that's what makes it so entertaining. If you like funny family sitcoms then this is definitely a watch for you!

All American

Genre: Drama

Rating: 7.5/10

This is a show that is based on a true story. The acting is very good and the storyline is very interesting. But it's one that you have to keep up with as well. This show is definitely one that you have to pay attention to, as it can be easy to miss some of the plot points if you don't pay attention. Overall, this show is very entertaining and worth watching. It's also very inspiring!

Victorious

Genre: Sitcom

Rating: 7/10

This is another one of my past-time and background noise shows. It is a fun show that is all about high schoolers who attend a performing arts High School. It's very good for pre-teens, teens, and even young adults can get into this show. Also includes music throughout the show which is also another huge bonus entertaining factor.

Found

Genre: Drama

Rating: 9.5/10

This is a show that I started watching when I came back to JMU at the beginning of this year. It's about a women who owns a business that finds missing people. They use their own methods instead of going to the police, which you might think isn't the best way to go about finding the person. But, the show goes into why those methods that they use are sometimes more effective that the policemens strategies. Very good acting, storyline, and very entertaining. This show is definetly one that keeps you on your toes, so if you like suspense this show is definetly for you.

Star

Genre: Drama

Rating: 8/10

This is a show that I started watching when I came back to JMU at the beginning of sophomore year. It's about a girl group that sings and faces challenges while trying to make their ways to the top of the industry. It involves plenty of drama and many surprising moments. If you like shows with a lot of drama that you have to keep up with then this is one for you. Also the soundtrack is amazing, so that adds to my rating as well because I love good music. The acting is also very good as well as the storyline.

Empire

Genre: Drama

Rating: 9/10

This is one of my absolute all time favorite shows. Reason being my favorite genre for shows is drama which this show falls under, it has some of my favorite actors and actresses, and the storyline isn't straightforward. When you initially watch you will think it's about a family dominating the music industry, but it goes into concepts surrounding family, faith, and loyalty. Not to mention, this shows soundtrack is also amazing. All of the actors and actresses have some sort of music talent so this also makes the show very intersting. The only thing that I have to say about this show is that towards the ending seasons, it started to seem dragged out. But overall is was amazing.

Griselda

Genre: Drama

Rating: 9/10

I stumbled across this show and I was immediately hooked. This show is about the life of a women named Griselda Blanco, a columbian drug lord who was very successful for a very long time. The acting was absolutely amazing and the actors played theor parts very well. This show also involves some action and suspense elements that keep you on the edge of your seats. If you're not really into drama or seeing crime, then this show probably wouldn't be your preference. But, if you love a good combination then this is definetly something I would pick for you to watch.

Nemesis

Genre: Drama

Rating: 8.5/10

This show is about two characters, one whose a cop and one whose a criminal. The characters eventually cross paths and realize that they have a very deep history. This show involves lots of action and suspenseful elements that contribute to its intrigue. I didn't like the way that the show ended, I felt like it was left open-ended but that's probably what they intented so that viewers can watch the next season. Last but not least, the actors and acting isn't perfect, but it's pretty good. I definetly would recommend watching this if you would like something to binge and you would like for it to include action as well as suspense and drama.

Love Island

Genre: Reality TV

Rating: 7/10

This is a reality TV show that has people from different backgrounds come together to find love, but in ways that you wouldn't think. The cast completes challenges and creates connections with other people on the island to see who they are compatible with. This is a very popular show and has a very large following/fanbase. If you are looking for something to watch that's both entertaining and suspensful I would recommend. The only thing I would say is it does involve a lot of physical romantic activity and it is very long. You also have to keep up with this show and cannot miss big portions of it or you will be lost in the next episode. Lastly, it's a very long series as well, which can be a bit overwhelming.

Movie Reviews

Twilight

Genre: Romance

Rating: 7/10

This is a movie that mixes fantasy with romance. The acting is also very good, which only makes the movie better. The storyline was very intersting and kept me engaged. It's definetly a movie that you have to pay attention to and cannot miss large chunks of it. Rather than this being on for background noise, this is something that you should actually watch. Very entertaining!

Mean Girls

Genre: Comedy

Rating: 8.5/10

I have seen this movie many time over and over again. This is a classic chick-flick that never gets old. It involves humor, heart, and has a message. This is a movie that I would recommend if you just want something fun to watch while you're relaxing or unwinding. It doesn't require a lot of concentration, and the acting is very good.

Karate Kid

Genre: Action/Drama

Rating: 9/10

This movie is a class take on the orginal movie The Karate Kid. It's Jaden Smith as the karate kid who is learning Karate due to him being bullied at his new school. Jackie Chan is the instructor, so from these two actors alone you know that it's a good movie. This is one of my favorite childhood movies, I used to love Jaden Smith as the Karate Kid and I just felt like the story was very inspiring. If you have previously seen the original movie then you will most likely enjoy this one too. Even if you haven't, if you like action and drama movies, you will definitely enjoy this one.

Princess and the Frog

Genre: Animation

Rating: 10/10

This is a beautiful animated movie that tells the story of a princess who is turned into a frog. The animation is stunning, and the story is heartwarming. It's a great movie for families to watch together. It's a timeless classic that everyone can enjoy from ages 5 to 100.

Hangover

Genre: Comedy

Rating: 8.9/10

This is a hilarious comedy that follows three friends who attend a bachelor party in Las Vegas. The movie is filled with outrageous situations and funny moments that will keep you laughing throughout the entire film. If you're more of a comedy person, then this is definitely the movie for you.

Why Did I Get Married Again?

Genre: Drama

Rating: 8.5/10

This is a movie about 5 couples who are going to see their friends daughter get married. The story explores themes of love, commitment, and the challenges of relationships. It's also got some humor in there as well. If you're a fan of Tyler Perry Movies or just funny comedies with a message, I would defeintly recommend this one for you.

Hercules

Genre: Animation

Rating: 9/10

This is a classic animated movie that tells the story of Hercules, the son of Zeus, who must prove himself a true hero. The animation is vibrant and the music is catchy. It's a fun and entertaining movie for all ages. If you love Disney movies, this is definitely one to watch.

Let It Shine

Genre: Musical/Drama

Rating: 10/10

This is a Disney Channel Original Movie that tells the story of a young man who is trying to find his voice and express himself through music. The movie has great music, a positive message, and is very entertaining. It's a must-watch for fans of musicals and inspirational stories.

Michael

Genre: Biographical Musical / Drama

Rating: 9/10

This is a biographical musical drama that tells the story of the life and career of the legendary musician Michael Jackson. The movie explores his rise to fame, his struggles, and his impact on the music industry. The acting is superb, and the music is unforgettable. If you're a fan of Michael Jackson or biographical films, this is definitely a must-watch. I absoluetely love this movie.

Gridiron Gang

Genre: Drama

Rating: 8/10

This is a drama film that tells the story of a group of juvenile delinquents who are sent to a military-style boot camp as an alternative to traditional juvenile detention. The movie explores themes of redemption, discipline, and the power of mentorship. It's a compelling watch for those interested in character-driven stories.
